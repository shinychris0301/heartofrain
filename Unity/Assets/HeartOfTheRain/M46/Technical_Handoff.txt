M46 TECHNICAL HANDOFF — 17 OCTOBER 2026

Architecture
BoardModel.cs owns occupancy, goals, move budget and resolution. Piece IDs
persist through movement. Each accepted action produces immutable before/after
snapshots and timed steps; the review locks interaction until those steps finish.
BoardPresentation.cs samples these steps and the approved power-up choreography.
M46Playground.cs maps the samples to pooled SpriteRenderers and review controls.
M46Assets.asset binds all textures, manifests and canonical level JSON.
M46AtlasBank creates frame sprites lazily, validates clip names when sampled,
and caches them. Original power-up source files remain available in M33 paths.
The adapted M46 planners use actual masks, occupants, colors and targets.

Board coordinates and art
Integer x grows right; y grows up. Cell pitch is 1 Unity unit. The canonical
boards have 64 pixels per cell plus 15 pixels of margin on each side. This
margin is retained; it is not squeezed into the cell pitch. Background faces
use the approved RainSlate_R2 alternation, with zero added gutters.
L10 is 9x9; L18 and L40 are 9x11; L41 is 11x11. No canonical level was resized.
The source grayscale masks are preserved. Four RGBA alpha-mask derivatives
encode exactly the same mask values into alpha for Unity SpriteMask use.
Body sprites are masked to the active-cell union; flights, beams and debris
can extend beyond it. Obstacles occupy cells exclusively, with no gem overlay.
Camera framing and safe-area layout are dynamic. The UI is a review interface.

Resolution order
Validate swap -> spend one move -> swap animation -> match/activate -> damage
and power chains -> jar collection -> vertical gravity/refill -> cascades ->
regrowth/reshuffle -> win/loss/input unlock. A single accepted move can produce
many cascades. Invalid swaps restore the exact state and use no move. Delivery
has its own phase; refill cannot enter a collecting jar's cell early.

Gravity and refill
Gems, power-ups, crates and jars fall vertically. Fixed obstacles and inactive
cells split each column into segments. Only segments connected to a declared
spawn point refill. Sealed cavities can therefore remain empty until their
blocking obstacle is removed. Diagonal slide, portals and conveyors are not
part of this milestone. Refill uses a deterministic xorshift PRNG; the earlier
low-bit LCG pattern that caused excessive cascades was removed. Resolution has
an explicit guard, which was not reached by the delivered verification cases.

Match creation
3 in line: normal clear. 4 horizontal/vertical: corresponding rocket.
5 or more in line: Rainbow Orb. Intersecting horizontal and vertical runs:
Bomb. 2x2 square: Rain Seeker. Priority is orb, bomb, rocket, square. The swapped
destination is preferred for creation. Canonical layouts were originally
validated for line matches; some contain dormant 2x2 groups. Only groups
intersecting newly swapped/fallen/refilled piece IDs resolve, so an unrelated
opening move does not clear a dormant group elsewhere. Revisit initial-board
square avoidance in the campaign authoring milestone.
Golden Seeker has no invented match recipe or unlock rule. It is review-placed
here and produces TWO Golden Seekers when activated. Targets prefer damageable
obstacles, then distant eligible gems. A delivery jar is never a seeker target.
When too few targets exist, excess children dissolve without inventing a hit.

Orb + Bomb            9      Orb + Orb             10
Rain + Rocket        12      Rain + Bomb           13
Rain + Rain          14      Orb + Rain            15

Standalone IDs: H4, V5, Orb7, Rain11, Golden16, Bomb21.
Golden + Golden is intentionally unsupported: no approved rule was supplied.
Double Orb keeps the approved Super Orb body design without a new Super Orb
mechanic. Existing M33 chain-reaction demonstrations remain in the source pack.
Vertical combinations normalize the partner's visual pose to the horizontally
authored merge sheets while retaining its actual reserved cell in the model.

Obstacle rules
Supply crate: HP1/2/3, falls; adjacent match or direct power-up damage.
Well overgrowth: HP1/2, fixed; adjacent match or direct power-up damage.
Rune stone: HP1/2/3, fixed; adjacent match or direct power-up damage.
Restoration jar: falls, immune to damage; only a marked delivery exit counts.
Sealed supply case: HP1/2, fixed; direct power-up damage only.
Water sluice: HP1/2, fixed; adjacent blue-water match or direct power-up damage.
One activation damages a given obstacle once. Independent seeker children and
converted power-ups have independent activation IDs. Final damage decreases
the goal once. The visual goal counter changes at body release (+0.24 seconds),
or at the jar collection timestamp. It never decrements on a mere HP crack.

Regrowth
Enabled by the level data from L27. After two consecutive moves without moss
damage, replace one eligible orthogonally adjacent gem with HP1 overgrowth.
Exclude powers, obstacles and exits. Choose bottom-left deterministically;
maximum three added clumps per level. Increase the remaining moss goal for each
new clump. Damage resets the quiet-move count. Clearing all moss prevents growth.

Visual effects
The 42 approved power atlas bindings and 86 obstacle clips are included.
Gem debris uses the approved M33 three variations per color, not a new random
fragment count. Obstacle pieces use the approved 1.3 scale and per-HP fragment
profiles. Pieces keep their baked volume and tumble; they do not fade or shrink.
Horizontal travel is contained in the viewport; despawn occurs only when the
entire sprite falls below the camera bottom. FX can outlive a logic step and
continue across gravity. Restart replaces the entire session and presentation
history. No delayed coroutine callbacks can mutate a restarted model.

Playback evidence
ExportPlayback.cs runs the same BoardModel and BoardPresentation used by Unity.
It exports state traces and compressed per-frame draw commands at 30 fps.
render_playback.py crops the approved atlases and composites those commands.
The offline compositor approximates the shader's edge glow; Unity shader/mask
behavior must still be checked. Complete videos preserve time and event order.
The short review uses cuts between timestamped excerpts, with no speed changes.

Validation boundaries
PASS: standalone C# compilation/execution; 280 ordered/directional combination
cases; 64 focused mechanics checks; 280 presentation cases and 3,872,610 sampled
poses with valid clip names; canonical board/asset provenance and frame bounds.
The four reference playbacks contain actual swaps, creation, chains, damage,
falls, refill, regrowth and delivery. L10 reaches Won with review-placed powers;
this is NOT a proof of solvability under natural play.
NOT RUN: Unity import/API compilation, Play Mode rendering/input, Android/iOS
builds, GPU profiling, 50-level win-rate/balance, exhaustive reshuffle cases.

Unity acceptance pass still required
- Import in a fresh 2D project, compile and run M46 > Validate Assets and Gameplay.
- Open the supplied scene, play each level, swap/tap and combine horizontally
  and vertically; verify no missing sprites, pink shaders or bad frame sizes.
- Pause and restart during a flight, a collection and refill; verify clean reset.
- Check portrait safe areas, both configured input backends and touch gestures.
- Verify mask clipping, white/gold beams, clockwise orb motion, two Golden
  carriers, obstacle palette and full-screen debris travel against the videos.
- Profile a target phone after the optional mobile preset. Do not treat the
  desktop all-assets review scene as the final mobile runtime architecture.

Memory and build preparation
Reference base atlas texels: power 736.0 MiB; obstacle 287.5 MiB, RGBA32.
ASTC 6x6 is approximately 114 MiB for those same base atlas dimensions, before
block rounding, bodies, surfaces and runtime overhead. This is an estimate,
not measured GPU use. The menu applies Android and iPhone importer overrides,
keeps full dimensions, and leaves masks uncompressed. A clear-overrides command
is supplied. Confirm target GPU format support and review compression artifacts.
A shipping build needs selective asset loading, smaller sprite caches, lower
per-frame allocations and platform profiling. No audio, haptics, monetization,
saving, progression UI or pre-level booster economy is included in this slice.

Reproduction
Install Python with Pillow and NumPy, ffmpeg, and a .NET 8 SDK. Set
M46_DOTNET_ROOT to the SDK directory if dotnet is not discoverable.
Run from the package root:
  python3 Source/compile_headless.py Mechanics
  python3 Source/compile_headless.py Combos
  python3 Source/compile_headless.py PresentationChecks
  python3 Source/compile_headless.py ExportPlayback
  python3 Source/render_playback.py
  python3 Source/prepare_unity.py
The headless UnityMathShim defines only the math types used by choreography.
It is outside Unity/Assets and is not a fake renderer or Unity compilation test.
SDK binaries and transfer helpers are not included. No proprietary reference
app footage or competitor assets are distributed.
