REFERENCE REVIEW — R2

Inspected both uploaded references directly:
- Screenshot 2026-10-24 042354.png: Continue panel; wallet 710; +20 moves; Play On 900; coin offer below.
- Screenshot 2026-10-24 042404.png: Shop overlay over the same dimmed board, visible wallet, offers/coin rows and close control.
The separate Out of moves notification is specified by the user; it is not visible in these two still images.

WHAT WAS MISSING IN R1
R1 jumped directly into a tall generic modal. The large empty medallion occupied the center, there was no character,
the wallet competed inside the card, and insufficient coins required a separate Get coins route.
The shop was a functional placeholder rather than a designed overlay.

R2 DESIGN
1. A short full-width Out of moves band appears over the dimmed board after the final turn resolves.
2. A compact wide Continue? panel replaces it. Dariya sits on the left; the move badge and short offer sit on the right.
3. The wallet sits above the panel. A single Play On + coin price action is visually dominant.
4. A Village Purse coin offer sits below. This uses the existing M63 coin-pouch illustration and sample coin SKU.
5. Play On checks the current balance. Enough coins requests a move purchase; insufficient coins opens Shop immediately.
   There is no second Get coins button/intermediate screen. The reference itself shows an underfunded continue panel,
   so Shop is opened by the player's Play On tap rather than forced open before they can see the offer.
6. The in-level Shop focuses the existing coin catalog and keeps the board dimmed behind it. Closing it restores Continue.
   A verified coin purchase returns to Continue; the player taps Play On again to spend coins on moves.

BRAND / CONTENT
Keep sapphire/slate, ivory, warm gold and green actions. Do not copy the reference game's characters, logo, purple
identity, fake discounts, or countdown. Dariya's new transparent portrait is derived from her approved M67R4 reference.
The character and four new UI surface vectors are isolated assets; no global protagonist or gameplay artwork is replaced.
This is the coin-focused in-level shop entry, not a replacement for the main Shop page's special-offer carousel or bundles.
Rocket and Bomb remain separate items in the full shop; this coin-only view introduces no new booster bundles.

The reference shows +20 for 900. Our preview retains the prior unapproved fixture of +5 for 900: these amounts are
configuration values, not a design asset or a commitment to match the reference game's economy. Bind the real quote.
The six dollar prices come from the existing M63 preview catalog, which is explicitly marked isPreview. The native
build must get localized product prices/availability from its actual store SDK; the preview has no real checkout.

RESPONSIVE / CLARITY
The portrait and badge are independent high-resolution PNGs. Text, balances, +N and prices remain live.
Phones use a stacked offer; landscape places the optional coin offer alongside it. The shop list scrolls independently.
Preserve all 4x slice corners and source dimensions; use the original transparent alpha, not a flattened screenshot.
All five app languages are supplied. No Japanese. Native Unity rendering still requires a current-project build review.
