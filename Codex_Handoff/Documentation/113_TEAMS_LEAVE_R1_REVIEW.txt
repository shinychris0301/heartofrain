Behavior
Member Team Info opens an overlay whose Cancel, Close and Back actions do not send a leave request. Explicit confirmation sends a version-bound request through the protected durable operation journal. Pending can be closed without cancelling or resubmitting. Unknown outcomes require checking the same operation; no success is inferred from timeouts. Deterministic guarded rejections are recorded atomically as recoverable receipts and are shown as failure only after current membership is confirmed. On confirmed exit the app refreshes membership, invalidates only former team-scoped data, routes to the appropriate current team or Find Team, and shows the short success toast when applicable. A hidden-page success toast is deferred in memory until a fresh no-team result; it can be lost on process restart. Life-help and mail rewards already earned remain owner-recoverable and progress/inventory/wallet are not reset.

The existing backend rule is preserved: a leader with other members must transfer ownership before leaving; a sole owner leaving closes that team. The server supplies the restriction and the solo closure consequence is explicitly presented in the confirmation. The new server token binds membership history, owner and member count so a delayed request cannot silently leave a rejoined membership or acquire a changed team-close consequence. Historic legacy command-only requests remain compatible but do not gain this guard. No schema upgrade, new fee or new cooldown was introduced.
