## Finding and prior review limitation

The actual 720 × 1600 phone Shop capture on checkpoint89 shows a flat dark strip in the upper 64 pixels. Its page decoration begins at the safe-area boundary while the display extends above it. The local Players and Teams page construction uses the same clipped-background pattern. The visible band is an exposed underlying background, rather than an intentional new header or damaged image asset.

The Home baseline already paints its artwork to the top of the display. The defect therefore must be checked by page and component; it is not accurate to describe every page as having the same root cause. Journey has an additional header decoration that needs to continue into the top inset. First-start artwork/shades, Settings dimming and connected online backgrounds/shades also require the same separation of decorative display coverage from safe-area controls.

Home's ordinary artwork and its events drawer are distinct paths: the artwork was already full display, but a later audit found that the drawer's dimmer stopped at the safe-area boundary. The same audit found the uncommon fallback `GameplayHUD.ShowLoss` dialog's dimmer under a safe-area parent. These are now included in the expanded repair and test scope without changing game outcomes or the ordinary continuation flow.

The earlier checkpoint89 phone review passed five specific country/ranking/Shop-note/provider-availability cases. Its Shop note capture still contains this band. Those accepted assertions did not establish full-display decoration coverage, and that scoped PASS is not whole-page visual certification. The original checkpoint89 review, images and receipts remain unchanged. The new `Baseline89` capture reproduces the issue on that exact installed APK, preserving the owner's profile and preferences; it is a reproduction, not acceptance of the repair.

## Implemented repair

`App/Home/Runtime/PageBackdrop90.cs` adds explicit full-canvas bounds for non-interactive decorative graphics. It transforms canvas corners into the existing graphic parent's coordinates, so the decoration can cover top, bottom and asymmetric side insets without changing its parent, sibling order or inherited fades. Only that decoration opts out of UI clipping and raycasts; `RecalculateClipping()` detaches its already registered clip relationship immediately. Interactive controls and scroll content retain their existing safe-area layout and masking.

The Journey header extension preserves the header's lower edge and its title/content alignment while continuing its decoration to the display's top and side edges. First-start cover cropping uses the extended artwork bounds. Modal dimmers cover the display through the same selected decorative path, preserving the dialog's existing order above its dimmer.

Missing identity, additional arguments, an unexpected path or unavailable/invalid trust blocks the run. Neither Hub nor the broker is terminated, and the pinned engine is unchanged.
