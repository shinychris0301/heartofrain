## Implemented changes

- `LeaderboardR3Layout118` is a reusable R3 rank-card component factory for Players, saved Region and Teams: fixed title/tabs/scope, scrollable podium and rows, pinned server self/team row, and unchanged shared dock. It uses R3 blue/cream/gold asset roles, distinct score icons, bounded names and 48-unit touch targets. The card and main-tab width now follows the R3 layout's 1,000/1,080 reference width; the scope follows 656/1,080, and the first lower rank starts at the specified 1,054/1,920 height. Narrow screen rows 4 and 5 fit above the pinned row at 360×640; taller screens expose more body space.
- `OnlineSocialSurface82` now uses the authored per-sprite nine-slice proof scale at the actual page scale. The extra R3 scale cancellation and corner multiplier gave tabs, cards, score plates and avatar frames inconsistent radii on the phone. The Editor guard verifies that rendered pixels-per-unit times `artScale` equals the source proof value. Original PNGs and GUIDs remain intact.
- `OnlinePlayersPage82` passes the measured Android top safe inset into the R3 layout. The header and scroll viewport compact by at most 28 logical units inside the safe-area root; the title stays nonnegative, and ranks 4 and 5 remain visible above the pinned self row at 360×640. The isolated Android harness now calls the same inset-aware R3 builder.
- Podium cards now include the R3 Level/Points labels, and team rows display their actual service-provided member count with a painted icon. Team Detail and badge selection use the circular red close disc and a 48-unit hit target. Invite reserves its header overhang in the grouped modal root and keeps the close art inside the screen edge.
- Friends default, Find Friends, Requests and Block have separate compositions. Requests/Block and ranking privacy are reachable from compact secondary controls. Online friend searches, request status and action recovery remain service-backed. When no service is configured, the tabs still navigate between truthful unavailable/empty states and never issue a write.
- The production `LeaderboardPage` now routes to the honest connected/offline surface when no managed service is available. `LeaderboardDemo.json` and its original `.meta` were moved into `App/Leaderboard/Editor`; `LeaderboardAssets.asset` no longer serializes the sample roster. The M61 review scene has an explicit Editor-only fixture switch, and R3 sample names/portraits are compiled only under `UNITY_EDITOR` with a false-by-default view flag. Team sample data was isolated similarly. No production ranking, team or friend list is generated from the visual fixtures.
- The rank-response validator now rejects decreasing scores and inconsistent scores for a shared rank. Ties retain their authoritative rank and medal; the R3 fixture uses ranks 1/2/3 for gold/silver/bronze instead of the prior all-rank-1 sample. Russian-formatted scores follow the selected locale, independent of the host PC's locale.
- The R3 Editor guard now checks the actual bound GUIDs, Sprite import mode, source-pixel limits for Default and Android, nine-slice borders on the used TA panels/buttons/tabs, and Simple/preserved-aspect portraits in rendered UGUI. These R3 source sprites currently have bilinear filtering and no mipmaps, as required by `PARITY_FIX.md`. This is a scoped exception to the earlier UI Clarity R1 policy, which deliberately gives detailed Daily Gift/Wellworks art mipmaps and trilinear filtering while keeping sliced panels without mipmaps. No global import-policy change was made; Android downscale quality still needs device inspection.
- World ranking is always available. My region is shown only for players with a previously saved region, and the page offers no country-selection control. An empty region is never guessed from device locale or IP.
- Team reward, invite, leave and detail UIs use a fixed modal root with grouped header/body/footer and scrolling list content. Chat/mail UGUI text has rich text disabled; known formatting tags are stripped for display while literal comparison brackets survive. Original service data remains unchanged. Close/back paths retain their caller and existing service actions.
- Follow-up phone captures exposed a shared modal-header offset: Reward/Invite decorations were low and their close discs far left/down; Leave showed the same defect. Reward and Invite now place the crest 39 units above the frame, and close art 9 units above its top near the right edge. Leave uses its reference-specific right inset. The common modal root reserves 40 units above the frame, so header art and touch targets move together. Close hit areas stay at least 80x48 and inside the safe frame.
