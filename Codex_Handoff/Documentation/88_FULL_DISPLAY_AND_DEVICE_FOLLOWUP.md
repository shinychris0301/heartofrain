## Additional tutorial defects

`App/FirstStart/Runtime/PracticeBoardView.cs` fed raw model steps directly to the renderer. Live gameplay compiles those steps through `BoardPlayback.Build`; raw gravity motions have zero duration. The native baseline reproduced nine invalid-position errors at the first fall frame: `TileFall.Position` divided 0/0, producing NaN coordinates. Tutorial playback now uses the shared compiler. This supplies timed falling paths without changing the authoritative board result. After that correction alone, nine gesture controls passed and two press/release-only cases still failed.

`App/FirstStart/Runtime/FirstStartPage.cs` also relied only on an intermediate Drag flag. A valid press/release displacement was discarded if no movement event was sampled. Release now also checks final displacement. Pointer ownership, cancellation, multitouch draining and the existing 0.35-cell guard are retained. Final native verification passes 319 checks across 11 gesture/animation cases, with 99 positively timed fall motions and 99 finite start/mid/end pose groups. Ordinary preferences and profile data remain unchanged.

The first88 candidate passed actual 200ms Match/Rocket swipes and Clear on the phone in an isolated tutorial profile. The final candidate additionally passes a subthreshold no-swap control, Match200ms swipe, Rocket600ms swipe, Clear tap and three explicit Next taps: seven real Android inputs. All three lessons solve in one turn each, and ordinary profile/preferences hashes match before, after and after normal relaunch. Actual Rocket/Clear solved OS captures are inspected. These results do not establish the cause of historical87's swipe failure or explain why Android did not report the Editor's invalid-position errors. Those historical failures remain recorded.
