Behavior and rules
Team members can send messages and request or help with lives. A server-authoritative request accepts up to five free donations, one per teammate, closes when full or after four hours and has a four-hour request cooldown. Closed requests with donated lives may be claimed up to available wallet capacity; leftovers remain owner-recoverable, including after leaving or team closure. The timer and rewards are controlled by the service. Explicit retries retain the same protected operation identity for an uncertain result; a subsequent distinct partial claim is a new operation. Message drafts and pending commands are retained on failed or uncertain sends. Animation feedback follows confirmed service receipts; it does not grant rewards. The actual approved settings are in Backend/config/team_help.json and the operator upgrade procedure is Backend/TEAM_HELP_UPGRADE110.txt.

Existing team access, report/block paths and navigation remain. The chat UI handles waiting, help, collection, full capacity, offline, errors and retry; a Saved Lives route preserves owner recovery. Mail explicitly reports unavailable because there is no live mailbox service. Online presence and peer portraits are not fabricated. User text is limited to the existing 1,000 Unicode scalar server limit. The supplied 60 fps video is a design preview, not a measured device performance result.
