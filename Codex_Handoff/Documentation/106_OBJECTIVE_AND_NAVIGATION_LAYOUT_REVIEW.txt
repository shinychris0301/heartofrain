WHAT CHANGED

Objective sprites previously shared 56 px rectangles but had different transparent
padding. In the actual 720 x 1600 render the visible crate/moss/stone/pail maxima
were 48/36/36/31 px. Static, measured source-alpha bounds now give every mapped
sprite a 58-unit visible maximum extent, preserving its original aspect ratio,
inside a 60 px masked artwork cell. The matched render measures 58 px for all four.
All nine mapped goal types are covered. No readable imports, runtime texture
readbacks, new sprite copies or asset GUID changes were introduced.

The sliced goal panel grows from 120 to 122 px and stays inside the actual painted blue
header, including its shadow. Header, moves, portrait, board and control bounds
are unchanged. Counters use a centered bounded row with the original 1.16 pulse
and 0.24-second timing; real glyphs/checks stay separated from artwork and bevels.
Collection flights target the new optical cell center. Goal counts, completion
sounds and gameplay/model/reward semantics are unchanged.

Shared navigation now uses five equal columns inset 8% from the rounded ends.
All icons share one size and baseline regardless of selection; approved artwork
and selected-frame aspect are preserved. The measured720px icon centers are
all 106.5 px from the bottom, versus 110 px for inactive icons and 132 px for Home
before. Icons are intentionally smaller to fit the framed bar and label space.
The dock position/reservation and navigation actions are unchanged.

Press feedback tints the frame and pulses the centered icon while the selected
frame and hit bounds remain fixed. This avoids shrinking a frame under its
unchanged label. Label spacing accounts for accented glyph overhang/shadows.
A label box below 10 logical units omits the visual word; the full accessible
name and action remain. This fallback includes very narrow or enlarged UI
fixtures; the reviewed default 720 px Home and tablet retain their labels.
One-time binding can fit dock nodes to an actual safe mask smaller than the
minimum page frame; no per-frame fit, alpha analysis or idle transform writes
were added.
