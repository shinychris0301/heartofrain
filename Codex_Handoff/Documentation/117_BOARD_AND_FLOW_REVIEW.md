# Review 117: board layouts, tile fitting and out-of-moves flow

Status: partial implementation with a separate staged campaign preview. The live M47 chapter assets and replay catalog have not been replaced; old 11-row levels therefore still exist in the current main project. Do not describe the nine-row campaign as shipped. Exact tested candidate inputs and bounded replay evidence are in `Verification/Evidence/Review117/RepairedCampaignTestSummary117.md` and its JSON.

## Implemented in the main project

- When a dead board can be reshuffled, the existing model permutation remains unchanged; the gem presentation moves a small distance inward, shrinks and fades, swaps to the new model colours while hidden, then returns to its cells. Fixed obstacles remain still and the existing 0.45 s phase remains. `ReshuffleVisual/Comparison.html` shows Editor GPU samples from a forced shuffle, not a natural device recording.
- Freshly exhausted moves present one brief notice while the quote loads, then one Continue view. The legacy loading fallback cannot flash before its prefab. Restore skips the notice, and closing cancels queued transitions. Existing currency, checkpoint, Shop and confirmed-loss logic remains authoritative.
- Original gem silhouettes retain their proportions, occupy more of the cell, and are bounded for sampled scaling and rotation. Cell backgrounds fill the board surface. Compare the archived Match Villains reference, pre/post-fit captures and staged boards in `BoardCandidateComparison/Comparison.html`; that is not pixel-for-pixel parity or physical-device certification.
- Exact original 50 level JSONs and board-graphics mappings are retained. Saved attempt resolution uses the saved config hash; the old graphics mapping is checked, and a build guard checks its source files. A new, default-false `safeSegmentBottom` rule can defer moss growth that would cap an active cell; original levels retain old rules. It is enabled only for levels 27/44 in the staged replacement.

## Campaign proposal, not activated

The user specified nine rows and varying 9?11 columns, distinct configurations, and approved rebalancing counts and safe moss growth. The 50 staged levels have 30 nine-column, ten ten-column and ten eleven-column boards; all have nine rows and 50 distinct mask-plus-obstacle configurations. Initial objective units change 2,098 to 1,510. The developer should validate human difficulty and first-time experience; bounded solver success does not establish balance. A reduction to nine rows alone does not increase tile size on a width-limited 11-column phone board.

The original early levels 1?9 produced settled gaps in model testing, and a draft level 17 could become geometrically unplayable. The staged levels reposition/rebalance fixed obstacles, keep top-entry spawns, preserve objective types, and use the opted-in moss rule where needed. Selected 280 bounded trials across the exact staged level hashes had 150 wins, zero observed settled gaps and zero discarded hazards; each level has a replay-verified win. This does not prove all states, difficulty, performance or accessibility. The earlier detected failures and drafts are preserved. Growth04 binds the L27/L44 level hashes to the staged files and observes regrowth without gaps in the sampled trials; the earlier Growth03 report lacked this binding.

Generated staged board surfaces/masks reuse approved artwork; the generator reconstructed all 50 original image pairs with zero pixel differences. A representative three-width Editor render checked staged image and data hashes. FirstStart practice boards remain separate; actual tutorial-to-level transition in the preview still requires validation.

## Physical preview

The isolated `com.heartoftherain.review117preview` build was installed on a realme RMX3938, Android 16/API 36, without replacing or reading the original app. Forced, memory-profile fixtures exercised level 11 (9 columns), 22 (10) and 21 (11): each showed a contained board, a timed shuffle presentation and one notice followed by an offer. The probe changed the model without updating its cached idle HUD; its notice frames show a stale move count and do not verify natural exhaustion or a zero-move counter. The future test-only probe was corrected after this build, but that correction is not in the installed APK. No FPS measurement or provider/service test was performed.

A separate unforced fresh profile completed the prologue and all three tutorial lessons, opened the level-one modal and started the 9x9 candidate; a real matching swap reduced the displayed move count from 26 to 25. This preview attempt remains active. `Verification/Evidence/Review117/DeviceComparison.html` and `DeviceRunSummary117.json` include the exact scope, image hashes, retained build warnings, failed run-as collection attempt and correction to a reset receipt which stopped only the separate preview process before tutorial interaction. The device utility now fences preview resets by process identity; the original game was not stopped or read.
