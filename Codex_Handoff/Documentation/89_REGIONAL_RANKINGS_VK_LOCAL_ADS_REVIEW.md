## Requested behavior and implementation

Leaderboard and Teams now expose Global and My region standings. Region is the player's explicitly selected **country**, with a searchable country picker and readable names. No GPS permission or precise location is requested. Country is not inferred from Russian language, a timezone or the device clock. It is a self-selected preference, not verified residence.

In configured managed-server mode the preference belongs to the authenticated internal player. It is private to the owner; public player profiles do not gain a country field. Regional queries use that saved preference and require country selection first. A client cannot request an arbitrary other player's country to bypass this scope. Local preview pages keep their demonstration status and their country preference is a local presentation preference; they do not become live backend rankings because the new tabs render.

Player scores continue to derive from server-verified completed levels. The existing opt-in, blocked-player and deleted-account filters apply before ranking and pagination. Equal scores share rank. Friends and the player's own team-member standings retain their separate scopes.

Team standings rank active teams by the verified completed levels of eligible, opted-in members. A team's region follows its **current leader's selected country**; this is stated in the page. The leader must opt into rankings. A blocked or inactive leader hides the team; blocked, inactive or non-opted-in members contribute neither score nor ranked member count for that viewer. Ties share rank across page boundaries. The existing endpoint for a player's own team-member rankings remains intact. These queries do not grant rewards or turn Rain Cup practice rivals into real competitors.

VK signup/link appears alongside the existing Google option in Save Progress. Google was already an unconfigured placeholder, not working Google authentication; it remains disabled with honest availability text. Local mode also keeps VK unavailable. In a configured managed account, VK becomes available only when the authenticated backend explicitly reports its provider configuration.

The VK flow preserves the managed guest's stable internal player ID, current wallet, progression and purchase ownership. The server creates a ten-minute, state-bound PKCE operation, opens VK's official authorization page in the external browser, exchanges the returned authorization code and checks authenticated `user_info` against the token response. The app obtains status from its authenticated backend, can reopen or cancel a pending operation, and refreshes the status on return. Secrets and provider tokens do not enter Unity. Names, avatars, email, phone and provider tokens are discarded rather than imported into the save.

The provider association is unique. A VK identity owned by another journey produces an explicit conflict; there is no automatic merge, wallet replacement or silent account switch. Linking is not cross-device account selection: opening an existing journey on another device still uses the established player ID and recovery code. The linked-account copy states this limitation. Account deletion removes provider associations and flows in the existing atomic deletion transaction. A delayed provider callback cannot restore a deleted account or dereference a removed flow.

Rewarded Shop video keeps the approved **400 coins**, **three credits per day** and **600-second cooldown after a confirmed credit**. Its quota day now follows the player's local calendar. Android supplies its system timezone ID and current offset; the server uses its own clock and IANA timezone data to determine local midnights, including 23-hour and 25-hour daylight-saving days. Country selection and ad calendar are independent.

Changing timezone cannot reset the current quota. A travel change waits until the current window ends, then follows the new local midnight; it does not impose an extra 24-hour lockout. Existing credits in the former UTC+10 window survive the upgrade and that window completes before the new calendar takes effect. An offset fallback supports older or unsupported timezone IDs, with the documented limitation that a changed offset must be reported to anticipate a DST change. A device clock change cannot advance the server's quota or cooldown.

Shop's policy note says **“Free rewards: 3 times a day”**, with localized equivalents. The fixed UTC+10 schedule and specific cooldown text are removed from the player-facing note. During a cooldown the status gives a brief availability message. Reservation, no fill, cancellation or an incomplete video consumes no reward quota and starts no cooldown. The existing durable claim transaction, receipt deduplication and restart reconciliation remain authoritative for coin credits.

## Corrections found during review

The first regional native run (`Native/Regional01`) passed 5,425 checks / 80 cases, but root visual review of its 320-wide Russian country-picker capture found the wrapped hint overlapping the search controls. The assertions did not cover that intersection. The final layout positions the search row from the measured wrapped hint height, then places the country rows and navigation beneath it. Native checks now measure preferred text height and test every relevant control pair for overlap as well as panel containment. The final regional run passes 11,665 checks / 80 cases. Regional01 remains an actual native **PASS with an uncovered visual defect**, not a relabelled native failure or final-source acceptance.

The five-locale ad-copy update also contained literal `?` write corruption in accented and Cyrillic policy/cooldown entries. The strings were restored as proper UTF-8 and semantic checks now inspect the policy and cooldown text in all five languages, including rejection of replacement question marks. The final ad UI/parser results cover the corrected source. This correction does not change reward quantities, cooldown or quota authority.

The initial VK transport trusted the type of the returned OAuth `state` too loosely: a non-ASCII string could cause `compare_digest` to raise and leave a flow Exchanging until expiry. The field now must match the exact 43-character ASCII URL-safe pattern before constant-time comparison. Malformed state produces terminal Failed and a new signup can retry.

The original per-read provider timeout also allowed a slowly streamed response to hold a worker repeatedly. Both provider requests now share a cancellable 15-second exchange budget, with a monotonic deadline checked before calls, after headers and for each delivered chunk. Provider bodies remain limited to 32 KiB, redirects are rejected, and malformed/deep JSON and transport failures produce sanitized failures. Native/game account credentials are sent only to the authenticated backend. A stale player session cannot read another owner's cached provider status, reopen its authorization URL or cancel its flow.

The full backend suite's first run exposed a timing race in an existing notification contention test: its writer could release before a delayed receiving thread actually attempted the transaction. The test harness now holds the real SQLite writer until the attempted receive completes, preserves the measured lock-hold/receive bounds, and adds a 2.1-second delayed-start control. Three targeted actual SQLite contention controls pass. No production notification or payment code was changed to make this assertion pass.

The first ad migration fixtures also needed test-only downgrade cleanup, and an initial region assertion expected the wrong rank after blocking. Those early focused diagnostics are not collected native or Android failures and must not be reported as such. The source-bound full-run failures described below remain retained.

During physical navigation, the host's read-only wait for `AppSettingsPage` timed out because that component exposes no visible-overlay property. Settings itself rendered correctly in the actual OS screenshot. The single actual tap was not repeated. Root reviewed the subsequent direct OS captures for Settings and Account instead. `Phone/Device01/Observer_Limit.json` retains this host-observation limitation; it is not relabelled as a gameplay or Settings rendering failure.

## Configuration and remaining launch gates

The regional service requires the existing explicit managed identity/social/backend setup. A local demo is not an online ranking. Country is a self-declared preference rather than anti-cheat residency verification. The completed backend/native fixtures cover filtering, pagination and country controls. Actual-device UI controls and country-unset behavior have been visually reviewed; ordinary Android acceptance against a configured hosted service remains open.

VK needs an issued client ID, registered HTTPS callback, owned/hosted backend and the setup in `Release/RuStore/VK_ID_SETUP.txt`. The callback is `/v1/identity/vk/callback`; provider configuration defaults disabled. Reverse proxies, WAFs, tracing and access logs must redact callback query strings and never retain authorization URLs, state, codes, verifiers or tokens. Actual configured Android consent/cancel/browser return/process death/recovery, no installed VK app, conflict and deletion acceptance remain open. Full VK sign-in selection for an existing journey is not delivered by this signup/link addition.

Advertising remains disabled by default. Publisher/unit registration, SDK/provider configuration, privacy information and actual video/device verification remain necessary; no advertising revenue is claimed. The configured capped client-reported completion mode remains forgeable and is not provider-verified impression evidence. Only the server claim can credit the fixed reward.

Hosted HTTPS/backend operations, production signing, configured RuStore product and payment verification, moderation/legal operations, physical-device performance and the pre-existing release gates remain open. This work does not remove the Unity splash or certify the entire app as smooth or release ready.
