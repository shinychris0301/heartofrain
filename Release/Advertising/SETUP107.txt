VERIFICATION STILL NEEDED BEFORE LIVE ACTIVATION

Use Yandex's official demo-unit harness for SDK playback checks; do not click
live ads during development. The current full-game profile locks the supplied
real IDs and is not a fake demo-traffic build. Perform controlled Android checks
for completed/incomplete/cancelled/no-fill/failed ads, duplicate callbacks,
background/token renewal, app termination and reconnection, identity change,
wallet refresh and recovery without double credit. Confirm local-midnight,
travel/DST and cooldown behavior against authoritative server time. Test current
phones/tablets and app upgrades with real hosting before production activation.

Yandex does not offer server-side reward verification. This integration is
sdk_client_reported_capped, not SSV: the backend owns the 400-coin amount,
quota, ownership, atomic credit and durable receipt, but cannot independently
prove an impression. A modified authenticated client can falsely report up to
1,200 coins/day. There is no fabricated provider signature or public trusted
grant webhook. Completed SDK callbacks are recorded before networking, claimed
idempotently by the server, then projected from a fresh authoritative wallet.
